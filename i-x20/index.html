Sharia-compliant solutions

Property finance
with principle.

Explore acquisition structures designed for Islamic investors seeking transparent alternatives to interest-based lending. The right route depends on the property, provider and your circumstances.

01

Murabaha

Cost-plus purchase. A finance provider acquires the property and sells it to you at an agreed price, with its profit margin disclosed upfront. Repayments are structured and known from the outset.

02

Diminishing Musharakah

Shared ownership. You and the provider purchase together. Over time, you buy the provider’s share while paying rent on the portion it still owns—until you own the property outright.

03

Ijarah

Lease-based finance. The provider owns the property and leases it to you for an agreed term. Depending on the arrangement, ownership may transfer at the end of the lease.

These are high-level descriptions, not financial, legal or religious advice. We can help you explore the options with suitable specialists.
